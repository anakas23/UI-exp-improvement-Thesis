STUDY ORGANIZATION SYSTEM

Schedule, tasks, and focus — in one interface.

ProductiveMind connects your calendar, course tasks, and a focus timer into one clear system, without manually juggling multiple tools.

  • TIMER Pomodoro timer with focus session tracking
  • TASKS Tasks grouped by course and due date
  • STATS Weekly overview of time spent
